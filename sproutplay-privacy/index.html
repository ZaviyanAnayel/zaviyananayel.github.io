Privacy Policy — SproutPlay

Effective date: November 24, 2026
Developer: Zaviyan LLC, Sheridan, Wyoming, USA
Contact: business@zaviyanllc.com

SproutPlay is a kids' learning-games app (Pre-School through 5th Grade). It is designed to be safe for children, and this policy explains in plain language what the app does — and does not do — with information.

1. We collect no personal information

SproutPlay collects, stores, transmits, and shares no personal information of any kind — not from children and not from parents. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no login, and no in-app purchases.

2. Everything stays on the device

When a child plays, the app saves only game progress locally on the device itself: the child's first name and age (entered by a parent during setup), game stars and scores, unlocked stickers, and parent settings such as sound and daily time limits. This information never leaves the device. The app requests no internet permission and makes no network connections.

3. No advertising, no tracking, no third parties

SproutPlay shows no ads, contains no advertising SDKs, uses no analytics, and shares nothing with any third party. There are no social features, no chat, and no links that take a child outside the app.

4. Children's privacy

SproutPlay is directed to children under 13. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete from our servers. Parents can delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If you have any question about children's privacy, contact us at business@zaviyanllc.com.

5. Permissions

SproutPlay requests no special permissions. It uses the device's built-in text-to-speech voice for spoken prompts; no voice audio is recorded or sent anywhere.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and the effective date above will be updated.
