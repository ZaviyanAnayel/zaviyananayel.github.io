Privacy Policy — Twin Trap

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Developer: Zaviyan LLC, Sheridan, Wyoming, USA
Contact: business@zaviyanllc.com

Twin Trap is a brain-training cup game. It collects, stores, transmits and shares no personal information of any kind. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no login, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics.

1. Everything stays on your device

Your player profile, level progress, stars, coins and trophies are saved only in the app's private on-device database and are never transmitted to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it. We have no copy and no way to recover it.

2. No permissions, no internet

The app requests no permissions and uses no internet connection. It plays original synthesized music and sound effects stored inside the app — no audio is recorded or sent anywhere.

3. No advertising, no tracking, no third parties

Twin Trap shows no ads, contains no advertising SDKs, uses no analytics, and shares nothing with any third party. There are no social features, no chat, and no links that take you outside the app.

4. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this URL, with a new effective date.
